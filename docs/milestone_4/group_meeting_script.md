# Group recording script

Record these parts separately, then assemble two short meetings. Bryan, Will,
and Guadalupe each use their own voice. This is a staged research scenario with
asynchronously recorded dialogue. The actions and results described below belong
to the scenario. They are not evaluation results for LabSync.

Use the [group presenter script and dry run](group_demo_script.md) when showing
the processed recordings. The [solo demo](demo_script.md) remains available.

## The shared story

We are a research team designing a fair comparison of an AI research assistant
with retrieval enabled and disabled. The first meeting establishes the experiment
and assigns preparation. The second records a design change and assigns the next
actions. The audience follows three people through one project.

## What each person records

Record four short clips, one for each of your turns below. Each turn has a
filename. Send Bryan the original clips with those names. Bryan assembles the
clips in numbered order within each meeting.

- Bryan records `m1-01-bryan`, `m1-04-bryan`, `m2-01-bryan`, and `m2-04-bryan`.
- Will records `m1-02-will`, `m1-05-will`, `m2-02-will`, and `m2-05-will`.
- Guadalupe records `m1-03-guadalupe`, `m1-06-guadalupe`,
  `m2-03-guadalupe`, and `m2-06-guadalupe`.

Voice Memos or another audio recorder is fine. M4A or WAV is convenient. Use the
same quiet room and microphone for all your clips. Speak naturally, leave about
one second of silence at each end, and read only the quoted dialogue. Keep each
commitment exactly as written for the first dry run. Each speaker should agree
to recording, processing, and inclusion in the demo before sharing clips.

The full dates below avoid ambiguity. They are example November 2026 deadlines.
If recording after these dates, agree on replacement future dates together and
update every corresponding line before recording.

## Meeting 1 - Research planning

Aim for about 60-90 seconds total. Export as `research-planning.m4a`.

### Turn 1 - Bryan - `m1-01-bryan.m4a`

> I'm Bryan Yang. Our research question is whether retrieval helps an AI
> research assistant answer questions accurately. We will compare retrieval
> enabled with retrieval disabled. We decided to use the same model and the
> same questions in both conditions so the comparison is fair.

### Turn 2 - Will - `m1-02-will.m4a`

> I'm Will Liu. The retrieval-disabled condition gives us a baseline. We will
> hold the model settings constant so we can isolate the effect of retrieval.

### Turn 3 - Guadalupe - `m1-03-guadalupe.m4a`

> I'm Guadalupe Cantera. Our rubric will assess factual accuracy and whether
> each answer is supported by the source paper. We will score both conditions
> using the same rubric.

### Turn 4 - Bryan - `m1-04-bryan.m4a`

> I will prepare twenty evaluation questions from public research papers by
> November 20, 2026.

### Turn 5 - Will - `m1-05-will.m4a`

> I will configure the retrieval-disabled baseline by November 21, 2026.

### Turn 6 - Guadalupe - `m1-06-guadalupe.m4a`

> I will draft the answer-scoring rubric by November 22, 2026.

### What to check after processing

Look for three commitments, with the right speaker, quoted evidence, and the
three stated deadlines. Generated titles can vary. The summary should capture
the same-model, same-questions comparison and the shared scoring rubric.

Speaker labels can vary too. Play a clip before assigning a name. Confirm each
speaker independently in this recording. A spoken introduction helps a person
check identity, but does not establish an app-account mapping.

## Meeting 2 - Experiment check-in

Aim for about 60-90 seconds total. Export as `experiment-check-in.m4a`.
This represents a later check-in in the staged story. Keep the actual recording
date in the app and use an editing label to show the story transition.

### Turn 1 - Bryan - `m2-01-bryan.m4a`

> I'm Bryan Yang. The twenty evaluation questions are ready. We found that
> several questions require evidence from more than one paper. Today we are
> updating the experiment plan to track those questions separately.

### Turn 2 - Will - `m2-02-will.m4a`

> I'm Will Liu. The retrieval-disabled baseline configuration is ready.
> We will keep the same model settings and question set in both conditions.
> We have collected no accuracy results yet.

### Turn 3 - Guadalupe - `m2-03-guadalupe.m4a`

> I'm Guadalupe Cantera. The scoring rubric is drafted. We decided to add a
> separate multi-paper category because a single overall score would hide
> differences between single-paper and multi-paper questions.

### Turn 4 - Bryan - `m2-04-bryan.m4a`

> I will annotate the reference answers with supporting passages by
> November 25, 2026.

### Turn 5 - Will - `m2-05-will.m4a`

> I will export the experiment configuration for reproducibility by
> November 26, 2026.

### Turn 6 - Guadalupe - `m2-06-guadalupe.m4a`

> I will label the multi-paper questions in the evaluation sheet by
> November 27, 2026.

### What to check after processing

Look for three new commitments with their stated deadlines. Confirm the three
speakers again for this recording. The summary should capture the added
multi-paper category and its rationale. There should be no invented accuracy
measurement or claim that either condition won.

## Bryan's assembly steps

1. Put meeting 1 clips 01-06 on the editor timeline, followed by a separate
   timeline for meeting 2 clips 01-06. Keep each meeting as its own export.
2. Trim the empty ends and leave a short natural pause between speakers. Keep
   voices at similar audible levels. Export audio with all voices audible in
   mono. Keep the original clips so you can replace an individual turn.
3. Listen through both exports. Check the order, names, complete commitment
   sentences, and dates. Avoid music, overlapping voices, or spoken turn numbers.
4. Save the exports somewhere accessible through the simulator's Files picker.
   In LabSync, open the project, tap **+ > Import Audio File**, choose the export,
   confirm everyone's permission, and tap **Upload & Process**.
5. Process a dry run before filming. Separately recorded clips may affect speaker
   clustering. Check every label against the audio and correct the mapping before
   showing assignment or approval. Keep the final exports unchanged for the take.

Recording** button does not select these new files. Use **Import Audio File**.
The recordings still need to be made by the team.
