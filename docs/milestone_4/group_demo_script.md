# Group research demo and dry run

Aim for 3-5 minutes. Scroll down while presenting. Prepare the recordings using
the [group meeting script](group_meeting_script.md). This guide uses imported
audio from three real speakers, assembled asynchronously into two staged
check-ins. The [solo guide](demo_script.md) is a separate presentation option.

## The user story

> I'm Bryan, working with Will and Guadalupe on a research experiment. Across
> meetings, we need to remember our decisions, see who committed to each action,
> and recover why the plan changed. I want my own next action in my inbox.

## Prepare before filming

Create a fresh project called **Research Evaluation - Group Dry Run 1**. Use a
new project for each rehearsal or take so earlier copies of these commitments
do not trigger duplicate detection. Invite Will and Guadalupe if they will use
their own accounts. They can appear as named speakers without app accounts.

Open **⋯ > Follow-through**. Enable automatic approval, task-owner email, and
Bryan's receive-email preference. Confirm Delivery shows **Live email**. Keep
Gmail signed in to **bryandyang@gmail.com** and check delivery during rehearsal.

Live email in this demo is scoped to Bryan. Leave teammate email receipt off
unless they separately agree to receive messages and the configured sender can
deliver to them. The current Resend test sender is restricted to its permitted
recipient. Three voices and three task owners can be demonstrated with only
Bryan receiving email.

Import **Research planning** using **+ > Import Audio File**, confirm permission,
and tap **Upload & Process**. Preserve the actual recording date. After processing,
open **Tasks > Review speaker names**. Listen to each speaker and confirm the
name. Connect Bryan's speaker to his own account under **App member (optional)**
and tap **Confirm name**. Only connect teammates to their actual accounts.

Automatic approval requires a confirmed member-account mapping for each speaker
in each recording. A name alone is insufficient. If a teammate has no account,
manually approve their correctly attributed task and describe that review
accurately. Check Bryan's automatic approval before using any manual approval.

Have meeting 1 and its first Bryan email ready before the final take. Keep
`experiment-check-in.m4a` ready to import on screen. Upload and process both
meetings during the rehearsal so you know what actually happens.

## 1. Tell - Three researchers, one experiment

**About 20 seconds. Show the project and its planning meeting.**

Say aloud

> Our team is comparing a research assistant with retrieval enabled and
> disabled. We need to keep our experiment consistent and follow through on
> the work each person takes on. These two staged check-ins use our own
> voices, recorded separately and assembled into meetings.

Optional label **Three researchers - two staged check-ins**

## 2. Show - AI captures the discussion and owners

**About 45 seconds. Open Research planning.**

Show **Summary**, then **Transcript**. Play a short section containing two
different voices. Open **Tasks** and show the three commitments, assignees,
deadlines, and supporting quotes. Mention any manual approval used for teammates.

Say aloud

> AI transcribed and summarized our discussion and extracted three
> commitments. I checked which voice belongs to each person. The tasks link
> back to their original words, including the deadlines we said aloud.

Optional label **AI transcription + speaker separation + summary + task extraction**

## 3. Show - Bryan gets the first follow-up

**About 25 seconds. Show Bryan's task, then the actual Gmail message.**

Say aloud after verifying approval and inbox arrival

> After I confirmed my speaker account, the app automatically approved my
> clear commitment and emailed it to me. Here is the task and its meeting
> evidence in my inbox.

Optional label **Rule-based approval + email delivery after AI extraction**

Return to LabSync. Keep the teammates' different assignments visible briefly.

## 4. Show - The next meeting changes the plan

**About 45 seconds, excluding processing.**

Import the second file into the same project as **Experiment check-in**.
Confirm permission and tap **Upload & Process**. Shorten the wait in editing
with a visible **Processing wait shortened** label. Add **Later check-in in
the staged scenario** for the transition.

Say aloud

> At our next check-in, preparation is ready and the team adds a separate
> category for questions that need multiple papers. Each person takes a
> different next action.

Open the processed summary and tasks. Confirm speaker names and actual account
mappings for this recording. Show the new commitments and November 25-27
deadlines. The earlier tasks remain available in the project.

Optional label **AI extracts new actions from a second meeting**

## 5. Show - Research progress and a new email

**About 30 seconds. Open bottom Tasks > To-do and filter to this project.**

Mark Bryan's earlier question-preparation task complete as part of the staged
story. Leave his reference-answer annotation task open. Show the second Bryan
email in Gmail once it arrives. There are two immediate commitment emails from
two approvals. This sequence does not demonstrate recurring reminders.

Say aloud

> I can see the preparation I finished and my next action from the later
> meeting. That new commitment has its own follow-up email.

## 6. Show - AI recovers why the plan changed

**About 40 seconds. Open Chat, select this project, and choose All meetings.**

Ask

> Why did we keep the same model and questions, and why did we add a multi-paper category?

Show the actual answer and open a citation. Check the cited evidence for each
part before narrating it as correct. The first rationale belongs to planning,
and the added category belongs to the check-in.

Say aloud

> AI retrieves evidence across our meetings and gives a cited answer. I can
> recover the reasoning behind both the original experiment and the change.

Optional label **AI retrieval + grounded answer + meeting citations**

## 7. Tell - Close with the research outcome

**About 20 seconds. Show the project task list.**

Say aloud

> We turned two team discussions into named research actions, deadlines,
> and my email follow-ups. We can also recover why our experiment plan
> changed, with the source discussion available to check.

If time permits, ask **Which condition achieved higher accuracy?** Show the
actual response. These recordings contain no accuracy results, so a supported
response should acknowledge that the evidence is missing.

## Dry run - Follow this once before recording

1. Assemble and listen to both exports. Check that all three voices and every
   deadline are intelligible. Set aside about 15-20 minutes for the first rehearsal,
   with additional time if processing or account setup needs attention.
2. In a fresh project, import planning. Verify the summary, speaker attribution,
   three intended commitments, dates, and evidence quotes.
3. Confirm each speaker independently. Verify Bryan's automatic approval and
   actual inbox delivery. Check teammate account mappings or manually review
   their tasks. Do not map another person's voice to Bryan's account.
4. Import the check-in. Verify the new category, three different commitments,
   dates, and second Bryan email. Confirm the speakers again.
5. Complete Bryan's earlier task and check that his next task remains open.
   Run the rationale question and inspect citations. Try the accuracy question
   and check that the answer acknowledges missing results.
6. Rehearse the presenter steps with a timer. Prepare meeting 1 again in a fresh
   final-take project. Keep the same two exports and use that project in Tasks
   and Chat. Aim for 3-5 minutes after shortening processing waits.

If automatic approval fails, check the current recording's confirmed account,
the project setting, the extracted quote/date, and possible duplicate tasks.
If only teammate tasks remain pending, check whether those speakers have actual
member accounts. If Bryan's email is missing, refresh Follow-through delivery
history and check Gmail spam. Provider acceptance alone does not establish inbox
arrival. Resolve the rehearsal issue before claiming that step in the video.
