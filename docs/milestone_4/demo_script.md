# Bryan's research demo and dry run

Aim for 3-5 minutes. Start at the top and scroll down as you go.
Use two short lab check-ins to show the research moving forward.
Each email is triggered by a newly approved commitment. The two check-ins
simulate research progress, with the actual recording dates shown in the app.

## The user story

> I'm Bryan, and for today, I want to be a researcher studying whether retrieval helps an AI assistant answer questions accurately. Across lab meetings, I need to remember the experiment plan, follow through on my commitments and recover why we made a particular research decision.

## Get ready

1. Create a fresh project called **Research Assistant Evaluation** for this take.
   Use your own voice and Bryan account for both recordings.
2. Open the project's **⋯ > Follow-through**. Keep all three switches on.
   These enable automatic approval, task-owner email, and your own email delivery.
   Tap **Done**. Older evaluation projects may still have saved Off settings.
   Reopen Follow-through and check that all three switches stayed on and Delivery
   shows **Live email**.
3. Record and process the **Lab planning** paragraph below before the final take.
   Open **Review speaker names**, connect your speaker label to your Bryan
   member account, and tap **Confirm name**. Check that the clear task is
   automatically approved and its email arrives at **bryandyang@gmail.com**.
4. In the simulator's **Safari**, open **mail.google.com** and sign in to
   **bryandyang@gmail.com** before recording. Keep the first task email ready.
   Switch between **LabSync** and **Safari** on the virtual iPhone to show the
   app and its real inbox email in the same recording.

Use **+**, enter the meeting name, confirm recording permission, then tap
**Record Audio > Start Recording**. When finished, tap **Stop Recording >
Use Recording > Upload & Process**.

### First recording, prepared before the demo

Name it **Lab planning** and read this paragraph.

> questions accurately. We decided to keep the same model and the same questions
> across conditions so the comparison is fair. I'm Bryan Yang. I will prepare
> twenty evaluation questions from public research papers by Nov (x)th.

## Your dry run

Run the preparation and steps 1-8 once at your own pace. Check these five things.

- Both recordings produce understandable transcripts and summaries.
- Each check-in produces its intended task, which is automatically approved
  after you confirm your speaker account.
- Each newly approved task sends its own email to your Gmail account.
- You can mark the first task complete and leave the second open.
- Chat cites the comparison rationale and avoids inventing experiment results.

For each repeat or final take, create a fresh research project and prepare its
first recording, task and email again. Add a short suffix to distinguish takes,
such as **Research Assistant Evaluation - Take 2**, and select that same project
in Tasks and Chat. Re-recording the same commitments in an existing project can
trigger duplicate checks and leave tasks pending.

Then repeat with a timer and aim for 3-5 minutes, using an edit to shorten the
processing wait. Keep automatic approval and email on throughout.

If a task stays pending, check **Review speaker names > App member (optional)**
is your Bryan account and tap **Confirm name**. Use the fresh project and the
scripted commitment without adding a deadline. If an email is missing, open
**⋯ > Follow-through > Refresh delivery history**, check the message status,
and check Gmail spam. Finish the dry run with both actual task emails visible
in your inbox before recording the final take.

Each email follows a newly approved task. Speaker confirmation and task
completion are your actions in this walkthrough. Ambiguous commitments can stay
pending for review. Keep the AI labels on screen as the corresponding feature appears.
