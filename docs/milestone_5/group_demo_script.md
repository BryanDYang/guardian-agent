# Three presenter demo script and dry run

Use this 4-5 minute app demo as a segment of the longer recorded final
presentation. The live showcase uses a separate spoken talk and one static
slide under the final assignment rules. See the
[final deliverables](roadmap.md#confirmed-course-deliverables).

Record together on Zoom for the final presentation. One person operates the app
and shares the simulator and email window, while Will, Guadalupe, and Bryan
speak in that order. Scroll down to your name for narration and handoffs.
Rehearse once, then capture a continuous take. Retake individual sections if
needed and edit them into the final video. Use headphones to reduce echo and
verify that the recording contains all three voices and the shared screen.

If scheduling prevents a shared session, record the three sections separately
using the clip names below, with one consistent app capture and matching audio
levels. The section times cover the app-demo segment. Add the problem,
architecture, evaluation, limitations, and future work for the complete final
presentation. The live one-slide showcase requires its own rehearsal.

This demo follows **Script #1** and **Script #2** in the shared
[project drafts document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1lAQ9qjxQD6AXb4aP3PBdIQMv7ctDbD47Re_STAPht28/edit).
Their scenario dates are **November 24** and **December 2, 2026**. The team records
these meeting conversations as audio for LabSync to process, then separately
records the three presenter clips below to demonstrate the outputs.

## Prepare together

- Pull the latest changes and start the app and backend.
- Accept invitations to the shared project. Check **Project ⋯ > People** for
  Will, Guadalupe, and Bryan with their actual accounts.
- Record the two scripts as separate audio files, with everyone's agreement to
  recording, processing, and inclusion in the demo. Label them as staged meetings.
- Upload meeting 1 using **+ > Import Audio File > Upload & Process**. Set its
  scenario date to November 24, 2026. Confirm speaker names and account mappings,
  then review and accept the tasks used in the presentation.
- Upload meeting 2 with scenario date December 2, 2026. Confirm its speakers
  separately. When recorded together on November 24, label this as a staged later
  check-in. Review the later candidates against the earlier accepted tasks.
- Keep Bryan's Gmail ready and verify an actual task email for this project.
  Teammate email delivery depends on their preferences and the configured sender.
- Find the examples below before filming. Generated titles can vary. Use the
  narration that matches the actual approval and review actions you performed.

## Will presents the team and meeting capture

**About 75 seconds, from 0:00 to 1:15.**

### Tell

Show the shared project.

> We're Will, Guadalupe, and Bryan. When our team meets, we assign work,
> change deadlines, and sometimes hand a task to someone else. We built
> LabSync to help us track those commitments and the discussion behind them.
> We'll show two staged team meetings using our own voices.

### Show

Open **Project ⋯ > People**. Show all three names and roles, then tap **Done**.
Open the November 24 meeting and show **Summary**.

> Here are the people in our shared project. We uploaded our planning
> discussion, and AI transcribed it and generated this summary.

Switch to **Transcript** and play about five seconds containing a speaker change.

> AI separates the speakers. We check the audio and confirm which project
> account belongs to each voice.

Use the editing label **AI transcription + speaker diarization + summary**
while those features appear. The People screen can have **Shared project members**.

### Tell and hand off

> Guadalupe will show how we review the actions extracted from that discussion.

End with meeting 1 open. Save your clip as `demo-01-will.mp4`.

## Guadalupe presents tasks and changes across meetings

**About 100 seconds, from 1:15 to 2:55.**

### Tell

Start in meeting 1's **Tasks** segment.

> A useful task needs an owner, a deadline, and evidence of what was said.
> AI extracts candidates, and we review them before relying on them.

### Show the first meeting

Find Will's transcript-evidence task or Guadalupe's error-analysis task. Show
its owner, deadline, and supporting quote. If speaker confirmation has not been
shown, briefly open **Review speaker names** and show the actual account mapping.

> This task links back to the original words. During our first meeting,
> we moved the transcript-evidence deadline from November 30 to December 1.
> We also moved the error-analysis deadline from December 1 to December 3.
> I can check what AI extracted against the discussion.

Show the actual dates. If a correction is needed, use **Edit** and describe that
correction. Tentative promises can require manual approval.

Use the label **AI task extraction + quoted evidence**.

### Show the second meeting

Open the December 2 meeting, briefly show **Summary**, then open **Tasks**.
Locate the Google login handoff to Bryan and the task-status deadline change.

> At our later check-in, Will hands the Google login work to Bryan, and
> the task-status deadline moves from December 2 to December 3. AI extracts
> the new information, and we review its relationship to our existing tasks.

Show one reviewed change. For a matching deadline-change candidate, use
**Update an existing task**, select the earlier accepted task, and confirm the
new deadline and evidence. Reassignment uses the accepted task's **Edit** assignee
control. Rehearse the exact candidate and available controls before filming.

> I confirm the existing task and its updated details so our project
> reflects the later discussion.

Use the label **Human-confirmed task update** during the confirmation.

### Tell and hand off

> Bryan will show follow-through and how we recover the reasoning across meetings.

End on the updated task. Save your clip as `demo-02-guadalupe.mp4`.

## Bryan presents email and cited answers

**About 105 seconds, from 2:55 to 4:40.**

### Tell

Open bottom **Tasks** and filter to the shared project. Show an accepted task
assigned to your account.

> Once a task is accepted, I can see it in my project task list and receive
> a follow-up in my inbox. Clear commitments can be automatically approved
> after speaker confirmation and the app's evidence checks.

### Show email follow-through

Switch to Gmail and open the matching task email. Show its task and meeting
context. Choose the narration that matches what actually happened.

For an automatically approved task, say

> This commitment passed the automatic checks after I confirmed my speaker
> account, and its follow-up arrived here.

For a manually approved task, say

> After I reviewed and approved this task, its follow-up arrived here.

Use the label **Accepted task → email follow-through**.

### Show a cited answer across meetings

Return to **Chat**, select the shared project, and choose **All** meetings. Ask

> Why did we move the error-analysis deadline from December 1 to December 3?

Open the answer's supporting citation. The discussion explains Guadalupe's
recording tests, date-extraction retest, and README review workload. Check that
the displayed answer and evidence support that explanation.

> AI retrieves evidence across our meetings and answers with a source
> citation. We can recover why a deadline changed and open the discussion
> to check it.

Use the label **AI retrieval + grounded answer + source citation**.

If time permits, ask **What final accuracy did our three-speaker evaluation achieve?**
The scripts contain no final numerical evaluation result. Show whether the
actual answer acknowledges that missing evidence.

### Tell and close

Show the task list or both meetings.

> Across two meetings, we can see who took on the work, review changes,
> receive follow-ups, and recover the reasoning behind our decisions.
> The original meeting evidence stays available for the team to check.

Save your clip as `demo-03-bryan.mp4`.

## Dry run and assembly

1. Verify all three accounts in People and speaker mappings for each recording.
2. Process meeting 1 first. Check task evidence and dates, then review and accept
   the tasks you plan to show.
3. Process meeting 2. Locate the handoff and deadline change. Rehearse the actual
   reviewed update once before recording the presenter clips.
4. Verify Bryan's real email and the rationale question with citations.
5. Read each person's narration against the app. Adjust claims that differ from
   the observed behavior.
6. Record the three clips with the same orientation and resolution, readable app
   text, and similar microphone levels. A face camera is optional.
7. Combine Will, Guadalupe, then Bryan. Shorten loading waits with a visible
   **Processing wait shortened** label if processing appears. Label the staged
   timeline. Aim for 4-5 minutes total.

checks and People simulator verification are recorded in the
